Soup

This is a simple and delicious soup recipe that can be made with a variety of vegetables and proteins. It's perfect for a quick weeknight meal or as a comforting dish on a cold day.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups chopped fresh tomatoes
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 large slice of onion
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons white sugar, or to taste
  • 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste

Steps

  1. In a large pot, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and garlic, and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in the flour and cook for 1-2 minutes, until it forms a roux.
  3. Gradually whisk in the chicken broth, making sure to break up any lumps of flour.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes, sugar, and salt. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 20-30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Use an immersion blender to puree the soup until smooth, or carefully transfer the soup to a blender and blend until smooth. Be cautious when blending hot liquids.
  6. Adjust seasoning with additional salt and sugar if needed. Serve hot, garnished with fresh herbs or a dollop of cream if desired.
